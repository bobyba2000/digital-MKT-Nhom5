About AURA

The Future of Personal Fragrance

Traditional perfume was designed for one scent, one mood, one moment. But modern lifestyles constantly change. You move between meetings, cafés, classes, social events, workouts, and late-night conversations — all in a single day. Your scent should evolve with you.

AURA introduces a new fragrance experience through a wearable smart bracelet that combines fashion, scent personalization, and lifestyle technology. Using refillable fragrance cartridges, adaptive scent diffusion, and app-based control, AURA allows users to personalize their scent experience based on mood, context, and daily routine.

Technology

Luxury Meets Wearable Technology

AURA combines fragrance craftsmanship with modern wearable innovation. Using adaptive micro-diffusion technology, AURA releases fragrance in subtle controlled pulses instead of overwhelming continuous diffusion.

This creates a softer, more refined scent experience while helping users maintain confidence and comfort across different environments.
