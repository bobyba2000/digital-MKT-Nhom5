# ABOUT AURA

## Section Title

The Future of Personal Fragrance

## Section Content

Traditional perfume was designed for one scent, one mood, one moment.

But modern lifestyles constantly change.

You move between meetings, cafés, classes, social events, workouts, and late-night conversations — all in a single day. Your scent should evolve with you.

AURA introduces a new fragrance experience through a wearable smart bracelet that combines fashion, scent personalization, and lifestyle technology.

Using refillable fragrance cartridges, adaptive scent diffusion, and app-based control, AURA allows users to personalize their scent experience based on mood, context, and daily routine.
